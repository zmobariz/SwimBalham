# Privacy policy

Swim Balham does not require an account and does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, telemetry, or personal information.

## Local data

The app stores preferences and cached public timetable information in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SwimBalham` for the current Windows user. This data stays on the user's computer and can be removed by deleting that folder.

## Network connections

Swim Balham connects to the following services:

- `opendata.leisurecloud.live` to retrieve public Places Leisure/OpenActive timetable data.
- `placesleisure.org` to enrich timetable information and when the user chooses to open an official centre page.
- `placesleisure.gladstonego.cloud` only when the user chooses to open a booking link.
- `ko-fi.com` only when the user chooses the optional **Buy me a coffee** link.

Those third-party services process requests under their own privacy policies. Swim Balham does not operate a server and does not receive data from those services about individual app users.

## Contact

Privacy questions can be raised through the project's [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/zmobariz/SwimBalham/issues).
